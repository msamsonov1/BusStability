Autonomous Microgrid Impedance Physics & Dynamic Inversion

In space exploration microgrids, tightly regulated point-of-load DC/DC converters feeding modern avionics, RF transmitters, and Hall-effect thrusters behave as Constant-Power Loads (CPL). Because dynamic current decreases when voltage increases to maintain constant power, their small-signal dynamic input impedance is negative, instigating limit-cycle oscillations and destructive blackout cascades.

1. Negative Dynamic Resistance Formulation

Consider a regulated converter consuming constant load power $P$. While the DC static resistance $R_{DC} = V_0 / I_0 > 0$ is positive, taking the small-signal differential Taylor expansion yields a negative incremental input resistance $r_{in}$:

$$P(t) = v(t) \cdot i(t) = \text{const} \implies i(t) = \frac{P}{v(t)}$$
$$r_{in} = \left( \left. \frac{di}{dv} \right|_{V_0} \right)^{-1} = \left( -\frac{P}{V_0^2} \right)^{-1} = -\frac{V_0^2}{P} = -R_{DC}$$

For a 120V bus delivering 3.6 kW to electric thruster inverters, $r_{in} = -(120)^2 / 3600 = -4.00\,\Omega$. When connected to input EMI filter stages, this introduces a negative damping term into the characteristic equation:

$$s^2 + s \left( \frac{R_L}{L} + \frac{1}{r_{in} C} \right) + \frac{1}{LC}\left(1 + \frac{R_L}{r_{in}}\right) = 0$$
If $r_{in} < 0$ and $|r_{in}| < L / (R_L C)$, poles migrate to the right-half plane ($s > 0$), instigating self-sustaining oscillations.

2. Middlebrook Impedance Stability Criterion

Partitioning the distributed spacecraft bus into a Thévenin source output impedance $Z_S(s)$ and a load input impedance $Z_L(s)$, the loaded bus transfer function $G_{bus}(s)$ relates to the unloaded transfer function $G_{src}(s)$ via the minor loop gain $T_m(s)$:

$$T_m(s) = \frac{Z_{source}(s)}{Z_{load}(s)}$$
$$G_{bus}(s) = G_{src}(s) \cdot \frac{1}{1 + \frac{Z_{source}(s)}{Z_{load}(s)}} = G_{src}(s) \cdot \frac{1}{1 + T_m(s)}$$

According to the Nyquist/Middlebrook criteria, stability and acceptable gain/phase margins are guaranteed for arbitrary load dynamic changes if and only if the locus of $T_m(j\omega)$ does not encircle $(-1, 0)$, satisfied conservative bound:

$$\|Z_{source}(j\omega)\| \ll \|Z_{load}(j\omega)\| \quad \forall \; \omega \in [0, \infty)$$
If peak source output filter impedance $\|Z_S(f_0)\|$ overlaps or approaches $\|Z_L(f_0)\|$, the system stability margin vanishes to 0 dB.

3. Non-Intrusive Online Micro-Chirp Observer

Passive hardware impedance sweeps are impossible during operational spaceflights. The PMAD firmware injects orthogonal pseudo-random wideband linear chirp currents $\tilde{i}_{inj}(t)$ at infinitesimal amplitudes ($<0.1\%$ bus ripple, unnoticeable to science instruments):

$$\tilde{i}_{inj}(t) = A_c \sin\left(2\pi \left( f_{start} + \frac{f_{stop}-f_{start}}{2 T_{sweep}} t \right) t \right)$$

High-speed DSPs compute the cross-spectral density between the synchronous bus voltage perturbation $\tilde{v}_{bus}$ and load current perturbation $\tilde{i}_{L}$ via short-time fast Fourier transform (ST-FFT):

$$Z_{load}(j\omega) = \frac{\mathcal{F}\{\tilde{v}_{bus}(t)\}}{\mathcal{F}\{\tilde{i}_{load}(t)\}} = \frac{S_{v i_{L}}(\omega)}{S_{i_{L} i_{L}}(\omega)}, \quad Z_{source}(j\omega) = -\frac{\mathcal{F}\{\tilde{v}_{bus}(t)\}}{\mathcal{F}\{\tilde{i}_{src}(t)\}}$$
Spectral updates occur every 25 milliseconds, tracking real-time degradation of electrolytic/ceramic capacitors over space radiation life.

4. Autonomous Active Damping & Loop Inversion

Instead of flying heavy, bulky physical passive RC damping networks that dissipate heat in vacuum, the software modifies the upstream Solar Array Regulator / Battery Discharger pulse-width modulation (PWM) control law with a virtual conductance $G_{virt}(s)$:

$$d_{PWM}(t) = d_{0}(t) - K_{damp} \cdot \mathcal{H}_{BP}(s) \cdot \tilde{v}_{bus}(t)$$
$$Z_{source,damped}(s) = \frac{Z_{source}(s)}{1 + G_{virt}(s) \cdot Z_{source}(s)}$$

By adaptively tuning the high-pass/band-pass filter $\mathcal{H}_{BP}(s)$ at the detected resonance frequency $f_0$, the peak impedance $\|Z_{source}(f_0)\|$ is suppressed by $>14\text{ dB}$, eliminating the negative real part of the dynamic admittance without introducing DC steady-state power dissipation.

Why Others Missed This: Legacy aerospace relies on massive, heavy passive electrolytic capacitor banks that add hundreds of kilograms of parasitic deadweight, severely slashing flight range. BusStability solves it purely in digital software with zero grams added.

2. Hyperscale AI Data Centers (48V Busbars)

Why Others Missed This: Data center operators continually swap out power supply units or over-provision busbar copper, ignoring the fundamental impedance overlap between server board voltage regulators and busbar filters.

3. EV Megawatt Charging Hubs

Physics in 1 Sentence: Simultaneous fast-charging of multiple semi-trucks causes dynamic impedance interactions that trip upstream medium-voltage solid-state transformers on nuisance harmonic overvoltage.

Why Others Missed This: Incumbents treat every charger as an isolated black box, failing to observe the shared microgrid resonance until the central breaker nuisance-trips under load.

4. Commercial Satellite Megaconstellations

Why Others Missed This: Satellite manufacturers validate impedance only on the ground at Beginning-of-Life (BOL); when satellites inexplicably reboot 3 years into orbit, they blame random single-event upsets instead of impedance collapse.
